# Interview with CFO — Q1 Budget Discussion

**2026-01-15 · 90 minutes · Marie (external consultant), Petra Novotná (CFO), Karel Dvořák (Warehouse Manager)**

**Marie:** Let's start with the day itself. Walk me through a normal morning.

**Karel:** I open SAP first, always. Transaction VA01 for the orders that came in
overnight. Then I have an Excel sheet where I track what actually left the
building, because SAP does not know that until someone posts it. And then the old
web portal for the partner deliveries.

**Marie:** Three systems.

**Karel:** Three systems. Nothing talks to anything. If I add up the typing, it's
two hours a day, every day, before I do anything that is actually my job. Let me
show you — this is the order, now I retype it here, and now again here.

**Marie:** And the portal, how does that hold up?

**Karel:** It falls over about twice a week. Not a crash you can plan around,
either — it takes whatever you were typing with it. I don't have the dates
written down anywhere, that's just what it feels like from sitting with it.

**Marie:** Worth checking against the IT logs.

**Karel:** Probably. Nobody has ever asked.

**Marie:** Petra, from your side — if there were a case for replacing the portal,
what does the money look like?

**Petra:** Not this half. We are in a freeze until Q3. I can fund discovery and I
can fund a pilot out of the operational line, but a capital request will not pass
before the Q3 review, whatever the business case says.

**Marie:** So the constraint is timing, not appetite.

**Petra:** Timing. The appetite is there. Bring me something costed by June and it
goes into the Q3 pack.
